# JOLKIRON

Floating solar and smart aquaculture for Bangladesh.

JOLKIRON puts solar panels on up to 30 percent of a closed pond or water body. The electricity runs aerators that keep fish alive at night, and the surplus goes to the grid. The rest of the water stays open for light, plankton, fishing, and boats.

The idea addresses two problems at once. Bangladesh faces night-time power shortages, and fish in ponds die when oxygen falls overnight. Floating solar on water also avoids taking farmland.

## Status

This project is at the concept and planning stage. There is no field data yet. The first step is a 0.5 MW pilot with rule-based aeration control, followed by a planned AI layer once about 12 months of pilot data exists.

The numbers in this repository are planning estimates. See `docs/CLAIMS_TO_VERIFY.md` for the list of values that still need sources or verification.

## Key numbers (planning estimates)

- 450 MW pilot slot: about 1,875 hectares of water surface (about 562.5 hectares of panels).
- 4,500 MW version: about 18,750 hectares, about 2.16 percent of closed water bodies in Bangladesh, and no farmland.
- 0.5 MW example: about 2.08 hectares of water, about 700 MWh per year, enough for about 290 homes.
- Estimated cost for 450 MW: about 4,050 crore taka.

## Scope and limits

This is not a bankable engineering or financial design. It has not been reviewed by a site survey, environmental assessment, or legal review. Electricity prices, exchange rates, interest rates, and yields are assumptions and must be updated.
