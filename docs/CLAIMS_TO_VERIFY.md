# Claims to verify before submission

Reviewers will check the numbers. Each item below needs a primary source link, a corrected value, or a clear label as an assumption. Remove any claim you cannot support.

- 11 August 2026 supply figures (18,975 MW demand, 15,833 MW supplied). Source: the specific news report or grid data.

## Assumptions to label clearly (not facts)

- Yield of 1,300, 1,400, and 1,500 kWh per kWp per year. Check against PVWatts or Bangladesh irradiance data before submission.
- Panel density of 0.8 MWp per hectare. Compare with published project data, which ranges from about 0.37 to 1.33.
- 30 percent cover limit. This is a design choice, not a cited standard. Say so.
- Construction cost per MW (7, 9, 12 crore taka). Replace with tender prices when available.
- Electricity price (8 to 14 taka per kWh). Check the current tariff.
- Degradation 0.5 percent a year, O&M 2 percent of capex a year.
- Household use of 200 kWh per month.
- Bank deposit rate of about 9 percent (estimated). Check current deposit rates.
- Projected returns (IRR of 8.2 percent for 0.5 MW and 12.3 percent for large projects). These come from the assumptions above. Show the model as an attachment if the form allows it.

## Claims that need softening

- "Zero fish mortality" is a target. Write it as a goal the pilot will test.
- "+20 to 30 percent fish production" is a target. Same treatment.
- "5 to 15 percent more electricity than rooftop" is an estimate based on temperature coefficients. Say it depends on site conditions and will be measured.
- "AI controls the aerators" overstates the current stage. The control logic is rule-based. Describe the AI as a planned layer on top of rule-based control, with machine learning to be tested after 12 months of pilot data.
- The "Anthropic science program" section should not claim any specific review process. Check the official program page for its actual requirements and eligibility.
